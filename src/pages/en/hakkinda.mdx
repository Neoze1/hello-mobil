# About OTOİZ

OTOİZ is an educational project that helps people explore car models and seller listings.

## Our purpose

We bring brands and models into one catalog to help you compare cars. Catalog models are separate from sale listings; missing prices and specifications are never invented.

## Developer

Ediz Davutoğlu · 2520171018. İstinye University, MYO063 Mobile Programming, Fall 2026–2027.

## First release scope

37 brands and 178 models; filters, favorites, comparison of three models, membership and photo listings. Payments, SMS verification and vehicle inspection are not available.

## App technologies

This desktop and mobile app is built with Tauri v2, Astro, Svelte 5, React, MDX and Rust.
